B. Midnight

Dark-first and soft-edged, like a premium card app at night. Figures glow off the dark without any actual glow. Questions answered right on Home.

  • Shape: 22px rounded panels, floating nav
  • Feels: sleek, modern, "app-like"
  • Risk: dark screens are harder for some older eyes in daylight; needs a light mode too

C. Blocks (chosen)

Bold colour fields, like a well-designed Scandinavian bank. Each kind of thing has its own colour: teal for cash, saffron for questions, plum for money set aside.

  • Type: Bricolage Grotesque for figures and headings, Figtree for text
  • Colour: warm stone page, deep teal, saffron, plum
  • Shape: 20px tiles, pill buttons and nav
  • Feels: warm, distinctive, easy to find your way
  • Risk: more colour to manage; tiles must stay few and meaningful
